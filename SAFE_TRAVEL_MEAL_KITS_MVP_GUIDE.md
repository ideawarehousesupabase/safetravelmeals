# Safe Travel Meal Kits Limited
## Journey Allergen Assurance Platform (JAAP) — MVP Specification & Feature Guide

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Core Innovation

**Safe Travel Meal Kits Limited** delivers single-allergen, sealed, cook-freeze travel meals to travellers with clinically confirmed food allergies at key journey touchpoints: arrival hotels, meeting venues, and airport departure legs.

The platform orchestrates three proprietary engines operating under the **Journey Allergen Assurance Protocol (JAAP)** (UK Patent Application **GB2617886.3**, TRL 4):

1. **The Exposure Engine:** Translates complex itineraries into an occasion-by-occasion timeline and scores the risk of unverified food exposure.
2. **The Run Scheduler:** Organises accepted meal occasions into cook-freeze production runs grouped into one of six allergen exclusion sets, respecting batch sizes (up to 80 meals, target 70) and mandatory clearance boundaries.
3. **The Chain-of-Custody Ledger:** Binds production clearance, rapid protein swab results, numbered tamper-evident seals, cold chain temperatures, and venue sign-offs into an append-only, SHA-256 hash-chained verifiable audit trail.

### 2. End-to-End Walkthrough Example: How It All Works

A business executive with a clinically diagnosed severe peanut and sesame allergy flies from Edinburgh Airport (EDI) to London Heathrow (LHR), checking into a Greenwich hotel for three nights with scheduled client meetings in Canary Wharf and Holborn. 

1. **Intake:** The traveller enters their flight numbers, hotel check-in/out times, meeting blocks, and allergen profile into the **Itinerary Planner** (or uploads via corporate travel CSV). Explicit health consent is logged.
2. **Exposure Scoring:** The deterministic **Exposure Engine** evaluates the trip and flags five vulnerable occasions where safe food is unverified:
   * A late arrival at 21:40 (hotel kitchen closed; high unverified food risk).
   * Three hotel breakfasts (shared buffet line prone to cross-contact).
   * A working lunch at an uncatered workshop.
3. **Exclusion Set Assignment:** The rule engine matches the profile to **Exclusion Set 2** (*Peanuts, Tree nuts, Sesame*). If a profile cannot be covered, the order is transparently refused rather than served on a "best-efforts" basis.
4. **Production Scheduling:** The **Run Scheduler** batches the meals into an exclusive-use commercial kitchen session. A documented deep-clean and line clearance are executed and signed by a Level 3 certified supervisor. Rapid allergen protein swabs verify non-detection before cooking starts.
5. **Sealing & Packaging:** Dishes from the culturally tailored cook-freeze menu (e.g., South Asian chicken breast with basmati rice and vegetables) are blast-frozen, packed in tamper-evident trays, and sealed with unique numbered security bands.
6. **Cold Chain Transport:** Meals are dispatched in temperature-monitored thermal boxes to the Greenwich hotel desk and City meeting reception via courier. Peak temperatures remain strictly within 0°C to 5°C.
7. **Reception & Release:** The hotel duty manager inspects the seal number, checks temperature integrity, and signs the receipt on a mobile web portal. Each morning, releasing a breakfast kit creates an auditable ledger entry.
8. **Cryptographic Verification:** Upon receipt, the traveller scans the kit's QR code. The app recomputes the SHA-256 hash sequence from line clearance to hotel handover, mathematically verifying that the seal is intact, the temperature was maintained, and no substitutions occurred.

### 3. Real Data Architecture (Firebase & Deterministic Engine)

The platform is powered by a real-time, isolated cloud data layer backed by an internal deterministic processing engine:

$$\text{User / CSV Input} \longrightarrow \text{Cloud Firestore } (\texttt{users/\{uid\}}) \longrightarrow \text{Processing Engine } (\texttt{processInput}) \longrightarrow \text{Derived Workspace} \longrightarrow \text{Dynamic UI}$$

* **Primary Input Only:** Users only provide raw travel legs, allergen selections, and dietary preferences.
* **Automated Derivations:** Downstream kits, batches, exclusion sets, production runs, custody ledgers, exception detections, and validation metrics are dynamically generated by `src/lib/processing/`.
* **Zero External AI Dependency:** The engine is purely deterministic rule-based code. The same input consistently produces the exact same audit trail.
* **Assessor Preview Mode:** For unauthenticated visitors and visa endorsers, an instant preview mode renders the pre-registered 150-movement dataset (§5.4) without requiring an account.

### 4. Page-by-Page Feature Directory

#### Group 1: Public & Account Entry Points
* **Home / Landing Page (`/`):** Explains the value proposition, highlights primary research statistics (64 travellers surveyed, 73.4% finding safe travel food difficult), presents the six fixed products, and showcases the 5 Core Endorsement Audit Pillars.
* **Onboarding & Authentication (`/onboarding`):** Provides secure signup and login via Firebase Auth with special category health data protections. Includes an instant **"Explore Demo as Assessor"** bypass button.
* **About & Contact (`/about`):** Details the founder’s background (hotel chef, Level 3 Food Hygiene, HACCP practitioner), technology leadership, and hosts a direct contact form written to Firestore.

#### Group 2: Traveller Core Experience
* **Dashboard (`/dashboard`):** Central command centre displaying upcoming trips, active meal-kit statuses, kit production counts, and delivered meal metrics derived directly from the user’s account.
* **Itinerary (`/itinerary`):** Intake builder for flights, hotel stays, and meetings. Runs `processInput()` live to show real-time exposure window generation, rule firing traces, and package recommendations.
* **Meal Plan (`/scheduling`):** Chronological timeline of mapped exposure occasions. Allows travellers to accept or remove individual meals and inspect kit IDs, production runs, and seal numbers.
* **Meal Menu (`/meals`):** Visual catalog of the cook-freeze travel menu across four cultural preferences (British, South Asian, East Asian, Middle Eastern) engineered to be eaten without a kitchen.
* **Kit Tracking (`/tracking`):** Live movement view from commercial kitchen dispatch to hotel front-desk handover. Features an interactive **"Recompute Hashes"** button validating SHA-256 chain continuity.
* **Sample Data & Import (`/import`):** Complete CSV ingestion utility for business accounts to upload multi-leg trips or operational events. Provides one-click loading and clearing of pre-registered validation datasets (§5.4).
* **Checkout & Plan Summary (`/checkout`):** Reviews the confirmed kit plan, handovers, and transparent tier pricing matching signed letters of intent (£25 to £600).

#### Group 3: The 5 Endorsement Audit Pillars (JAAP Protocol)
* **1. Workflow (`/protocol`):** Interactive 10-stage visual workflow diagram (Stages 1–10) detailing stage inputs, outputs, checks, and fail-states. Includes an interactive failure simulation slider.
* **2. Rule Matrix (`/rules`):** Complete auditable rule base: Exposure rules (`R1–R4`), Exclusion Assignment (`R5`), Production Run Batching (`P1–P2`), and Handover (`H1–H3`) with live execution evaluation traces.
* **3. Kit Records (`/records` & `/records/$kitId`):** Searchable register of 150 simulated meal-kit records. The detail view (`/records/$kitId`) exposes the granular SHA-256 cryptographic hash-chain ledger (`prevHash` $\rightarrow$ `hash`).
* **4. Validation (`/validation`):** Real-time gate dashboard measuring the 6 pre-registered validation endpoints for the Month 4 Gate Decision (§5.4 & §6.3): zero swab detections, $\ge 98\%$ ledger completeness, $\ge 95\%$ on-window delivery, $\ge 98\%$ temperature integrity, $\ge 99\%$ seal integrity, and 0 reaction incidents.
* **5. Exceptions (`/exceptions`):** Comprehensive register of 7 distinct operational failure scenarios (`EXC-01` to `EXC-07`), demonstrating how the protocol quarantines runs, withdraws kits, and writes tamper-evident corrective entries.

#### Group 4: Operations & Management
* **Kitchen Runs (`/kitchen`):** Back-of-house operational sheet for partner commercial kitchens. Tracks line clearance deep cleans, competent supervisor sign-offs, and swab testing.
* **Hotel Handover (`/hotel`):** Mobile-first front-desk interface for hotel duty managers to log incoming kit batches, record storage temperatures, check seals, and log guest releases.
* **Overview / Admin (`/admin`):** Operations dashboard displaying weekly kit production volumes, completed handovers, active production runs, and capacity constraints.
